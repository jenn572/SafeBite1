Our app is called safebite. 
It is an app that helps people who want to eat healthier
or,with allergies, and those with dietary or religious
restrictions to know what they're consuming and what's in their food.
Our main feature is the scanner which allows the users to 
scan multiple items at once, not only that but also a sort of alarm 
or sign which will inform users if there is anything to look
out for in the scanned item.
